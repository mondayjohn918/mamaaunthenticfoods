The menu

Choose your rice, choose your soup, add your protein. Tap "Add to order" on anything you want and send it all to us at the end.

Rice

Ask for stew or sauce with any rice, then add a protein below.

Jollof rice

Long-grain rice cooked down in pepper and tomato until it picks up that smoky party flavour.

Fried rice

Rice tossed with mixed vegetables, liver and seasoning, bright and savoury.

White rice

Plain, fluffy rice to take any stew or sauce you like.

Soups

Soups are served with your choice of swallow. Ask what we have today.

Egusi soup

Ground melon seed simmered with leafy greens, palm oil and assorted meat.

Vegetable soup

Ugu and waterleaf cooked down rich, the everyday favourite.

Ogbono soup

Ground wild-mango seed, thick and slippery, with stockfish and meat.

Okro soup

Fresh chopped okro with pepper, palm oil and seafood or meat.

Ofada soup

Green pepper and locust bean sauce, fiery and deep, made for ofada rice.

Bitter leaf soup

Washed bitter leaf in a thick cocoyam base, the eastern classic.

Proteins

Fried chicken and beef, cooked fresh and kept warm in the front glass case. Tell us how many pieces when you order.

A neighbourhood kitchen with a mother's hand.

We cook the food we grew up eating, the way our mothers made it.

Mama Authentic Foods started with a simple idea: the food at a roadside kitchen should taste as good as Sunday lunch at home. So we start early, buy fresh at the market, and cook our soups slowly in big pots, the same way every day.

You can eat in, take a plate home, or call ahead so it's ready when you arrive. When you have a big day coming, we'll cook for your guests too.

How we cook

Fresh every morning

Soups are made the same day they are served. When a pot runs out, it runs out.

No shortcuts on flavour

Real palm oil, locust beans, stockfish and properly washed leaves. Ofada sauce is ground, not bottled.

Clean and covered

Food stays in a covered glass display, served hot with clean utensils, every time.
